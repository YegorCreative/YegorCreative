How close is this to Photoshop?
  • Mean and Std Dev use the same math as Photoshop's Histogram panel. RGB uses all red, green and blue values of the selection together (Photoshop's default). Luminosity weights them 30% red, 59% green, 11% blue.
  • For sRGB JPEGs the numbers usually match Photoshop within about ±1. Photos saved in Adobe RGB or other color spaces may be converted by the browser and differ more, so shoot or export in sRGB.
  • Raw files (.NEF, .CR2, .ARW…) can't be opened in a browser. Measure them in Photoshop/Camera Raw as the lab says, or export them as JPEG first (the export adds processing, so the numbers won't match the lab's raw measurement exactly).
  • For your graded lab, report the Photoshop values your professor asks for, and use this tool to check them and make the graphs faster.
