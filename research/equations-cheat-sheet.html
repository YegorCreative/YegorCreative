Equations Cheat Sheet

Transmittance
transmitted light / incident light
100 lux in, 50 lux out → 50 / 100 = 0.5
Opacity
incident light / transmitted light
or 1 / transmittance
100 / 50 = 2
Density
log(opacity)
or log(1 / transmittance)
log(2) = 0.3 · each ×2 in opacity adds +0.3 · messy numbers: write log(200 lux / 85 lux)
Neutral Density
0.3 = 1 stop
0.6 = 2 stops · 0.9 = 3 stops · 1.2 = 4 stops
Inverse Square Law
E = I / d²
85 lux at 15 ft → 30 ft (2× farther): 85 / 2² = 21.25 lux · point source only
Mired Scale
(1 / color temperature) × 10⁶
2000K → 1,000,000 / 2000 = 500 · higher K = lower mired
F-number
focal length / diameter of aperture
100 mm / 10 mm = f/10
Luminance Ratio
highlight EV − shadow EV
EV 8 − EV 4 = 4 stops
On the exam: write the formula, plug in the numbers with units, then the answer. No calculators, so if the math is messy, stop at the setup.
