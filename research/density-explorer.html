Density, Opacity, Transmittance

1. Drag the slider to make the filter darker

ClearDark
TRANSMITTANCE = out ÷ in
"how much gets THROUGH"
OPACITY = in ÷ out
"how much it BLOCKS"
DENSITY = log(opacity)
"how DARK"

Watch: as the filter gets darker, density and opacity go UP together (twins). Transmittance goes DOWN (the opposite).

3. Why density is "linear and additive"

Stack a 1-stop filter and a 2-stop filter together:

Filter AFilter BTogether
Density
just ADD ✅		0.3+0.6=0.9
Transmittance
must MULTIPLY ❌		0.5×0.25=0.125

"Density Does the adding." That's why photographers use density.
