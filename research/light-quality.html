Flux vs intensity

All the light, or light in one direction?

FluxThe rate a source emits light in ALL directionsLumens (lm)

Think of the full amount of light pouring out of the bulb everywhere at once.

IntensityLight coming from a source in a SINGLE directionCandelas (cd)

Only the light heading one way, like toward your subject. It's the I in E = I / d².

Fluxlumens

total light, all directions

Intensitycandelas

Illuminancelux

light landing on a surface

💡
Flux = Full amount, everywhere. Intensity = In one direction. Trap on the test: "Flux measures light in one direction" is False.

02

CRI: Color Rendering Index

The classic score for how true colors look

Exam answerCRI = Color Rendering Index. A perfect CRI is 100 (not 80).
What it measuresHow accurately a light shows colors compared with a reference light of the same color temperature
Scale0 to 100 (100 = perfect)
Samples8 pastel color samples, averaged into one number (Ra)
For photo & video90+ is what professionals look for
WeaknessOnly 8 soft colors and one number. It barely tests saturated reds (important for skin), so two lights with the same CRI can look quite different.
💡
CRI is a report card for color, and 100 is a perfect score. Related: lights that act like a black body get a color temperature, and lights that don't (fluorescent, LED) get a correlated color temperature (CCT).
03

TM-30: Rf and Rg

The newer, more detailed color rating from the IES (first published 2015)

Rf · FidelityHow ACCURATE are the colors?0 to 100 · perfect = 100

The upgraded version of CRI. It uses 99 color samples instead of 8.

Rg · GamutHow SATURATED are the colors?about 60 to 140 · neutral = 100

Above 100 = more saturated · 100 = same as reference · below 100 = duller

Rf · FIDELITY
Rg · GAMUT

Example lights for learning, not measurements of real products.

Color vector graphic: the dashed circle is the reference light. The colored shape is the test light. Outside the circle = more saturated, inside = duller.

💡
Rf = Fidelity = Faithful (accurate, max 100). Rg = Gamut = Glow (saturation, 100 = normal). Always read them together: Rf 85 with Rg 110 means fairly accurate but a bit oversaturated.
