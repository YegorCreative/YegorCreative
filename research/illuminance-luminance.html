Illuminance vs Luminance

Memory trick

Illuminance = Incident = Incoming

All three start with "I" and all mean light falling onto the subject. Luminance is light bouncing off the subject toward the camera.

Sort the terms

Light falling onto a subjectLight reflected off a subject
IlluminanceLuminance
Incident meter reading (meter at the subject, pointed at the light)Reflected meter reading (camera's meter, pointed at the subject)
Not fooled by white or black subjectsCalibrated to 18% gray: white → underexposed, black → overexposed
